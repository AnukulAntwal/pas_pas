Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 27 October 2025

1. Introduction

PasPasPackage (“Pas-Pas”, “we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app or website.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, residential address, Aadhar card, driving license.
  • Usage Data: Device information, app version, interaction activity.
  • Location Data: Pickup and delivery locations for matching and route generation.
  • Payment Information: Required only for platform service fees.

3. Why We Collect This Information

  • To verify users and maintain trust on the platform.
  • To match package senders with travelers or service providers.
  • To communicate booking updates and notifications.
  • To improve safety, service quality, and user experience.
  • To process platform service fees (if applicable).

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Facilitate communication between users.
  • Display relevant package or travel listings.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or suspicious activity.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared only in the following cases:

  • With payment gateway providers for secure transaction processing.
  • When required to comply with legal obligations or law enforcement requests.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by applicable laws.

7. Your Rights

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements).

8. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures including encryption and secure storage. However, no digital platform is completely secure, and users share information at their own risk.

10. Children’s Privacy

Pas-Pas is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.
